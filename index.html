          01 / About
          
Shreya - The ultimate glue between the two systems

        


        

          

            

              Most of my work lives in the seam between a product and the
              outside services it depends on — insurers, gateways, calculators.
              That seam is where things quietly break, so I've spent three years
              making it boring instead: predictable, tested, documented.
            

            

              I started self‑taught — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, then React — and
              learned the rest on the job. At Novacred Insurance I picked up

              product across four more. At The R.D. Group I moved fast across
              seven client projects, owning design, build, and testing end to
